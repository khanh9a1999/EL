**Bài tập ôn tập Ngày 1 → 20 (làm lại)**

Bạn gửi từng phần cũng được. Mình chỉ chấm từ, không bắt lỗi viết hoa, và ghi kèm mỗi câu thuộc ngày số mấy.

---

**Phần A — Điền vào chỗ trống**

1. I ___ a developer.  => I am a developer  => Nghề nghiệp
2. They ___ from Vietnam.  => They are from Vietnam => Nơi chốn
3. ___ she your friend?  => Is she your friend? => Danh từ - Xung quanh một người 
4. Where ___ you from?  => Where are you from? => Hỏi nơi chốn
5. He ___ to work by bicycle every day. => He goes to work by bicycle every day => HDT vì có every day - he/she/it + Ve,es - phủ định do,does thêm n't
6. My wife ___ coffee in the morning. => My wife drinks coffee in the morning => HTD - my wifle = she
7. I ___ drink tea.  => I like drinking tea 'or' I like to drink tea => Nếu là like to + V-ing hoặc like + V-ing
8. ___ he work on Sunday?  => Does he work on Sunday => Câu hỏi Do/Does + N 
9. I wake up ___ 7.  => I wake up at 7 => at + time
10. I study English ___ the evening.  => I study English in the evening
11. I don't work ___ Sunday.  => I don't work on Sunday => HTD + do/does phủ định + nguyên mẫu và On + ngày
12. I am very ___ because I didn't sleep last night.  => I am very tired because I didn't sleep last night => HTD + QKD phủ định
13. There ___ a computer on my desk.  => There is a computer on my desk => tobe - There is/số ít , are/số nhiều
14. There ___ three books in my bag.  => There are three books in my bag
15. My phone is ___ my bag.  => My phone is in my bag => Vị trí in - trong , on - trên
16. The cat is ___ the table.  => The cat is on the table.

17. I ___ drive a car, but I ___ fly.  => I can drive a car , but i can't fly => động từ khuyết thiếu và cấy trúc thích/muốn
- can/n't + V
- like love + V-ing hoặc to V
- want/need + to V
- listent + to

18. I want ___ learn English.  => I want study learn English
19. She ___ music.  => She listen to music
20. I have ___ apple and ___ banana.  => I have an apple and a banana
21. I have a laptop. ___ laptop is new.  => I have a laptop. The laptop is new => CÂu đầu đã nhắc đến laptop nên câu sau dùng The luôn
22. ___ are my keys. (ở xa)  => Those are my keys
23. ___ is my phone. (ở gần)  => This is my phone
24. This is ___ car. (của anh ấy)  => This is he car
25. This is ___ bag. (của vợ tôi)  => This is my wife's bag => Tính từ sở hữu: *my, your, his, her, our, their* của người thì thêm 's
26. Look! They ___ football.  => Look! They are playing football.
27. I usually ___ up at 7, but today I ___ at home.  I usually wake up at 7, but today I am at home => usually - HDT cho thói quen, HDTD cho những việc đang làm
28. I ___ tired yesterday. => I was tired yesterday => QKD động từ tobe => I she he it - số ít thì tobe = was
29. ___ you at home last night? => were you at home last night? 
30. I ___ to the office yesterday. => I went to the office yesterday?  
31. She ___ TV last night.  => She was watch TV last night => Thói quen Tobe
32. I ___ go to school yesterday.  => I didn't go to school yesterday? Phủ định của QKD
33. ___ you work last week? => Did you work last week? => Đặt câu hỏi của thì quá khứ + Do -> did + S + V

**Phần B — Sửa lỗi (mỗi câu có 1 lỗi)**

34. I am from VietNam.  => I am from Vietnam => Sai tên
35. My job is develop.  => My job is a developer => Thiếu a
36. She don't like coffee.  => She doesn't like coffee
37. Are you work on Monday?  Do you work on Monday? => chỉ công việc và câu hỏi do/does 
38. There is two chairs next to me.  => There are two chairs next to me
39. They needs help. => They need help
40. I can to swim.  => I can swim.
41. I don't like an onion.  => i don't like onions => Onion là danh từ đếm được nên và câu này nói chung nên thêm s
42. That are my keys.  => Those are my keys => Số nhiều + Tính từ sở hữu đứng danh từ
43. This is she bag.  => This is her bag => tính từ sở hữu
44. Are you study English now?  => Are you studying English now? => thì hiện tại tiếp diễn, nói về hành động đang xảy ra.
45. She usually drink coffee.  => She usually drinks coffee => She + ... + V/s
46. I was go to school yesterday.  => I went to school yesterday.
47. I went to home yesterday.  => I went home yesterday - Về nhà thì home thôi, ở nhà thì at home
48. I didn't went to work.  => I did't go to the work => V nguyên mẫu
49. Did you worked yesterday?  => Did you work yesterday => V nguyên mẫu
50. Did he was busy? => Did he were busy?

**Phần C — Đổi câu theo yêu cầu**

51. She works on Monday. → phủ định  => She doesn't work on Monday 
52. You like coffee. → câu hỏi  => Do you like coffee?
53. He is busy. → câu hỏi  => Is he busy?
54. They are at home. → phủ định  => They don't at home
55. I work now. → hiện tại tiếp diễn  => I am working now
56. I am tired. → quá khứ  => I was tired
57. We go to the office. → quá khứ  => We was to the office
58. He had breakfast. → phủ định  => He had breakfast
59. They watched TV. → câu hỏi  => Did they watched TV
60. Did you go home yesterday? → trả lời ngắn phủ định => Yes, I did
